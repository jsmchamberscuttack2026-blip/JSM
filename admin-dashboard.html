• ON (Live Mode): Public website is fully active. Visitors can view chamber information, legal monographs, opinions, and submit consultation bookings.
• OFF (Lockdown / Under Repair Mode): The entire public website blanks out all chamber data and renders the official "Website Under Scheduled Repair & Security Maintenance" screen with repairing symbols (🛠️ ⚙️ 🛡️) and error notices.
